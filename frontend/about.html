Our Story

From One Truck to a Trusted Fleet

Noko Transport was founded with a simple mission: provide reliable, professional heavy-duty transport that businesses can count on. What started as a single truck operation has grown into a dedicated two-truck Code 14 fleet serving clients across Johannesburg and beyond.

Our investment in technology — including this online booking platform — reflects our commitment to making transport logistics as seamless as possible for our customers.

🎯

Mission

To deliver safe, timely, and cost-effective transport services with our Code 14 fleet that exceed customer expectations every time.

👁️

Vision

To be the most trusted name in heavy-duty transport, known for reliability, transparency, and exceptional service.

💎

Values

Integrity, safety, punctuality, and customer-first service guide every decision we make.
